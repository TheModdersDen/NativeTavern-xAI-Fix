**NativeTavern v0.1.16 (Build 40)**

**Character card compatibility**
• Fixed Android character lists failing after importing community cards whose numeric fields were serialized as strings.
• Existing characters, chats, character books, and Live2D settings remain recoverable; do not clear app data to work around this issue.
• Improved compatibility with community character-card exports.
